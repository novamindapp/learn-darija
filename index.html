How to read Darija

Darija is usually written with Latin letters. Numbers stand for sounds English doesn't have:

3 = a deep "a" from the throat   7 = a breathy "h"   9 = a "q" from the back of the throat   kh = as in "loch"   gh = like a French "r"

Example: 3afak starts with that deep throat sound, then "fak". Say it slowly, then faster.

Mini dialogue: at the souk

All the phrases from this page, in one real exchange. Drahem means Moroccan dirhams.

You: Hello! How much is this?
Salam! Bsh7al hada?
سلام! بشحال هادا؟
Seller: 100 dirhams.
B 100 drahem.
بـ 100 درهم.
You: Too expensive! Lower it a little.
Ghali bezaf! Nqes shwiya.
غالي بزاف! نقص شوية.
Seller: Okay, 80 dirhams.
Wakha, 80 drahem.
واخا، 80 درهم.
You: Okay, give me this. Thank you!
Safi, 3tini hada. Shukran!
صافي، عطيني هادا. شكرا!
